The problem

Most dictation apps edit you.

They pipe your voice to a server, run it through an AI that "improves" your phrasing, and hand back something you didn't quite say.

Cloud dictation

Your voice goes to someone else's computer

Audio is uploaded, transcribed remotely, then rewritten by a language model told to make you sound better. You get polish you didn't ask for — and you pay monthly, forever, for the privilege.

Hands Free

Your voice never leaves the machine

Whisper runs on your Apple GPU. The cleanup model runs in the same app. There is no server, no API key and no account — the only time it touches the network is to download its models once.

Rewrites are rejected automatically. If the model strays, you get your own words back.
Verbatim by design

Allowed to punctuate. Not allowed to reword.

The cleanup model may only delete fillers and add punctuation. Every result is checked against what you actually said — and thrown away if it drifts.

What you said

saturday was a nice sunny day and in the afternoon early afternoon we went for a nice walk with Ellie to San Isidro we took one book to put in the book box of book house then we walked through San Isidro and we had a brownie

What gets typed

Saturday was a nice sunny day, and in the afternoon, early afternoon, we went for a nice walk with Ellie to San Isidro. We took one book to put in the book box of the book house. Then we walked through San Isidro and we had a brownie.

Same words, in the same order. Only punctuation and capitalisation were added — highlighted above.

Three levels of smartness

Pick how hard it thinks.

One slider in the menu bar. Move it any time — your choice is remembered.

Fastest

Whisper Turbo in a single pass, plus punctuation and capitals. Text is there almost before you let go.

For quick replies, chats and searches.

Balanced

Retries the hard bits, then a small local AI model removes the ums and false starts — without changing your words.

The default. Great for almost everything.

Accurate

The full Whisper large-v3 model plus cleanup. A little slower, noticeably better with accents, names and mumbling.

For long thinking-out-loud sessions.
Your words, kept

Everything you say, saved for you. Only for you.

Every dictation is added to one plain-text file on your Mac, with the date and time. Nothing is lost, nothing is uploaded — and that record turns out to be valuable.

Train your own models

Months of your real speech, in your own words — ready-made data for fine-tuning.

Write your own documentation

The ideas you explained out loud become notes, specs and docs you can mine later.

Give AI context about you

Hand the file to any assistant so it knows how you think, what you work on and how you talk.

What you get

Fast, quiet, and entirely yours.

Hold-to-talk

Hold Control+Option anywhere, speak, release. The text appears where your cursor already is — no window to open, no button to find.

Nothing leaves your Mac

Transcription and cleanup both run on-device. Works on a plane, in a hospital, under an NDA. Audio is never stored or uploaded — only your text, in a file on your Mac.

A second, not a wait

Whisper runs on the Apple GPU and the cleanup model adds about a second. You speak, you release, it's there.

Guarded against rewrites

Output is rejected if it invents words, reorders your phrasing, or drops what you said. When in doubt, you get the plain version — never a hallucination.

Hear it start

A soft bubble pop the instant the microphone opens. Eyes on something else? You'll still know it's listening.

Always there

Starts at login and waits in the menu bar using nothing until you hold the key. Your last few dictations stay one click from the clipboard.

Setup

Three steps, then forget it exists.

Drag it to Applications

One app bundle with everything inside. No Python, no Homebrew, no command line, no separate AI server to babysit.

Say yes twice

macOS asks permission to watch for your hotkey and to type for you. The app walks you to the exact settings pane and tells you the moment it's ready.

Hold ⌃⌥ and talk

First launch downloads the models once. After that it's instant, offline, and out of your way forever.

Opening it the first time: Hands Free is free and not sold through Apple, so macOS warns you once. Click Done, then System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. Or paste this into Terminal: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Hands Free.app"
